A synthetic incident for trying Survey Anything without pointing a camera at a fire.

● REC CAM 2 ·
Quiet scene.
  1. Load the extension, accept the consent screen, and in Options pick the Fire or smoke rule and your alert channel.
  2. Keep this tab in front, open the extension's popup and choose Watch this tab.
  3. Press Start incident in 10 s. The on-device fire detector sees the flames; your vision model (if configured) confirms; the alert reaches you. With no vision model the alert says unconfirmed.
